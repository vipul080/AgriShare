AgriShare

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

AgriShare lets farmers rent farm machines from other farmers nearby. This policy explains what information the AgriShare app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, optional email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Location: the location you choose for yourself and for each machine you list, and — only when you tap "Use my location" — your device's current location, used to show machines near you. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Listings: machine name, category, price, description, address and photos you upload.
  • Bookings and reviews: dates, amounts, status, notes, ratings and comments.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by Razorpay. We store only the Razorpay order and payment IDs, never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Notifications: if you use the Android app, a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so we can send booking updates to your phone.
  • Language preference: so the app and notifications appear in your language.

How we use it

  • To show machines near you and let owners list their machines.
  • To run bookings: sending requests, confirmations, reminders and receipts.
  • To let the two farmers contact each other. Your phone number is shown to the other farmer only after a booking is confirmed.
  • To show ratings so farmers can trust each other.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the service working.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other farmer in a booking: your name, and your phone number once confirmed.
  • Everyone: your name, your listings (with their approximate location) and reviews are public so that farmers can browse.
  • Service providers: Razorpay (payments), Google Firebase (push notifications), OpenStreetMap (map tiles), and our hosting provider — only as needed to run AgriShare.
  • Authorities: when required by Indian law.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Access to the database is restricted.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account in the app, or at the account deletion page. Deleting removes your name, phone number, email, location, device token and takes down your listings. Records of past bookings and reviews are kept in anonymised form ("Deleted user") so the other farmer keeps their history and for accounting.

Children

AgriShare is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
