1. What KisanShare is — and is not

KisanShare is a place where farmers can list their farm machines and find and book machines from other farmers nearby. We only connect the two farmers.

  • We do not own, rent out, inspect, repair, insure or operate any machine.
  • The rental agreement is between the owner and the renter. We are not a party to it.
  • We do not check that listings, machines or farmers are what they say they are, though we may remove anything that looks wrong.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Give your real name and your own mobile number. One account per person.
  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens in your account.

3. If you list a machine (owners)

  • You must own the machine or have the right to rent it out.
  • Describe it honestly — model, condition, price per day, and whether a driver or operator comes with it.
  • Keep it safe to use and in working order. Machines used on public roads (such as tractors and trolleys) must have the registration, insurance and papers the law requires.
  • Only accept bookings you can honour. If you must cancel a confirmed booking, do it as early as possible so the renter can find another machine.

4. If you book a machine (renters)

  • Use the machine carefully, only for the work agreed with the owner, and return it on time in the same condition.
  • Only operate a machine if you know how to, or use the owner's operator. Driving on public roads needs a valid driving licence.
  • Follow all safety instructions from the owner.

5. Prices, payments and cancellations

  • The owner sets the price. The total is shown before you book.
  • Cash: you pay the owner directly. KisanShare does not handle this money.
  • Online (when available): payments are processed by Razorpay. The money is held when you book and only taken when the owner accepts. If the owner declines, or does not answer in time, the hold is released. If a confirmed online booking is cancelled before it starts, the payment is refunded. Banks can take a few working days to show the money back.
  • You can cancel a booking before it starts. An owner can cancel a confirmed booking before it starts.
  • Fees: any KisanShare fee is always shown before you pay. Cash bookings have no KisanShare fee. Owners may choose to pay for a "Featured" listing; this is optional.

6. Damage, accidents and disputes

  • Any damage, loss, injury or disagreement about a rental is between the owner and the renter. Before handing over a machine we suggest you both check it together, take photos, and agree on any deposit, fuel and repair costs.
  • If something goes wrong, contact the other farmer first. You can also write to us — we may help, but we are not responsible for settling the dispute.
